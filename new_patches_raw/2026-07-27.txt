Software needed for pre-patching:
* TrueAncestor_SELF_Resigner_v1.96
* HxD
1) decrypt eboot.bin -> eboot.elf (TrueAncestor)
2) edit/patch eboot.elf (HxD)
3) resign new patched eboot.elf back to eboot.bin (TrueAncestor)
4) replace the patched eboot.bin where it is executed

How to find the executed eboot.bin:
* If the game has updates odds are you will find the executed eboot.bin in the ps3 hdd: /dev_hdd0/game/???????/USRDIR/EBOOT.BIN
* If the game doesn't have update most likely the executed eboot.bin will be in the disc backup: /dev_hdd0/GAMES/??????/PS3_GAME/USRDIR/EBOOT.BIN

Side note on disc backups: if you prefer your backups to be .iso format: backup to folder format, patch the eboot.bin and after that you can convert it to .iso

Side notes on addresses:
"File address" = "Memory address" - 10000
Pre-patching uses "File address".
Cheat Codes use "Memory address".
